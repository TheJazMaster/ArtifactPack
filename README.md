# Artifact Pack
A Cobalt Core mod
<img width="708" height="202" alt="69_1757005891" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ab5f09fb-ba76-44bf-806a-b63c8bc2ce45" />

Adds more basic artifacts to the game to fill some design gaps:
- **Life Preserver**: Choose a card in your deck that isn't buoyant. It gains buoyant.
- **Flash Drive**: Draw 2 extra cards on the first turn. (Riggs isn't monopolising draw on my watch)
- **Material Upgrade**: Permanently gain +2 max hull and +1 max shield on pickup.
- **Dodge Engine**: Gain 3 evade on the fourth turn.
- **Card Refund**: Each time you exhaust a card, draw 1 more card next turn.
- **Incinerator**: On pickup, remove up to 2 cards from your deck, then gain a non-temp Fumes.
- **Mega-Thrusters** (boss): Gain 1 ace on the first turn. On pickup, add a wing to the far left of your ship.
- **Brain Drain** (boss): Draw +2 cards every turn. At the end of each turn, exhaust the cheapest card in hand.
- **Torment Nexus** (boss): On pickup, replace all basic cards in your deck with random upgraded cards from your crewmates.

Art by Soggoru Waffle (who did an incredible job), coding and concepts by myself.
